Common questions

Questions customers ask before booking.

How fast do you reach my location?
In our primary zone (East of Kailash, Nehru Place, Okhla, Lajpat Nagar, South Extension), we target 30 minutes. If we miss this window, your first service is free. Traffic and time of day affect this, so we always confirm an ETA when you book.
Do you come to basement and apartment parking?
Yes — most of our calls are from apartment basements. Please share the parking entry gate details, your floor or slot number, and any visitor access code when you book. This avoids delays at entry.
How do I pay? Do you take UPI?
We collect payment in person after the service is complete. We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), cash, and cards. No advance payment required at all.
Can you tell me if my battery actually needs replacement?
Yes — that is actually the most important thing we do. We run a state-of-health test first and show you the exact numbers. We will only recommend replacement if the battery is genuinely below a usable threshold.
Do you service bike batteries and two-wheelers?
Yes, we service car, bike, and inverter batteries. Just mention the type of vehicle when booking so the technician brings the right equipment.
What if I'm outside your listed areas?
Call us at 9311211157 or WhatsApp us. We occasionally service areas adjacent to our coverage zones depending on availability. We'll confirm within a few minutes.
